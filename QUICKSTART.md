# ChoreSync Quick Start Guide

Welcome to ChoreSync! This guide will help you get started with the application quickly.

## What is ChoreSync?

ChoreSync is a household chore management application designed for apartment renters and roommates. It helps you organize, track, and distribute household chores efficiently with real-time updates across devices.

## Getting Started

### 1. Registration and Login

1. **Create an Account**:
   - Click "Register" on the login page
   - Enter your full name, email, username, and password
   - Choose a profile color (this helps identify you in the app)
   - Click "Create Account"

2. **Login**:
   - Enter your username and password
   - Click "Login"

### 2. Setting Up Your Household

1. **Create a New Household**:
   - After logging in, you'll be prompted to create or join a household
   - Click "Create New Household"
   - Enter a name for your household
   - Optionally add a description
   - Click "Create"

2. **Invite Roommates** (optional):
   - Go to the "Household" tab
   - Click "Invite Member"
   - Enter their email address
   - Click "Send Invite"

### 3. Managing Chores

1. **Add a New Chore**:
   - Click the "+" button in the bottom navigation bar
   - Enter the chore name and description
   - Select a category (cleaning, maintenance, etc.)
   - Set the due date
   - Assign to a household member (or yourself)
   - Set frequency if it's a recurring chore
   - Click "Create Chore"

2. **View Your Chores**:
   - Go to "My Chores" tab to see chores assigned to you
   - "Today's Chores" shows what's due today
   - "Upcoming" shows future chores

3. **Complete a Chore**:
   - Find the chore in your list
   - Click the checkbox or "Mark Complete" button
   - Optionally add a comment

### 4. Using the Calendar View

1. **Access Calendar**:
   - Go to the "Calendar" tab
   - View all household chores organized by date

2. **Filter the Calendar**:
   - Use the filter options to show only specific members' chores
   - Filter by chore category or status

### 5. Checking Statistics

1. **View Dashboard**:
   - Go to the "Dashboard" tab
   - See completion rates and distribution of chores
   - Track your household's progress over time

2. **Personal Statistics**:
   - Go to "My Chores" tab
   - Check your personal completion rate and history

## Tips for Success

1. **Be Consistent**: Mark chores as complete as soon as you finish them
2. **Check Notifications**: The app will notify you about upcoming and overdue chores
3. **Use the Calendar**: It helps visualize the chore schedule for the entire household
4. **Communicate**: Use the comment feature to leave notes about specific chores

## Troubleshooting

- **Can't Log In?**: Make sure your username and password are correct
- **Chores Not Updating?**: Refresh the page or check your internet connection
- **Notification Issues?**: Check your browser settings to ensure notifications are enabled

## Need Help?

If you have any questions or need assistance, please refer to the full documentation or contact support.
